THE PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Three Crippling Bottlenecks of Modern Waste Systems

Municipalities spend up to 50% of operating budgets on waste collection, yet over 33% remains mismanaged, creating ecological hazards, carbon surges, and severe economic losses.

38% Contamination

Purity Degradation & Pulp Ruin

Citizens unknowingly discard food-greased cardboard (pizza boxes) or non-recyclable multi-layer foil packaging into clean streams. A single contaminated item ruins entire 5-ton pulping batches, forcing secondary diversion directly into landfills.

Impact: \$180 Billion in recyclable commodity value discarded annually.
42% Wasted Fuel

Static, Blind Collection Routes

Municipal diesel compactor fleets run fixed calendar-based schedules regardless of actual fill levels. Trucks visit half-empty bins, burning fuel and emitting heavy greenhouse gases, while overflowing bins in busy sectors cause curb spillage and pest infestations.

Impact: Over 1.6 gigatons of CO₂e emitted by municipal collection fleets.
1,400+ Fires / Year

Lithium-Ion & E-Waste Thermal Hazards

Small electronic toys, vape pens, and swollen smartphone lithium-ion batteries end up undetected in municipal compactor bins. The crushing force of hydraulic truck compactors ruptures battery cells, sparking catastrophic fires on transit routes.

Impact: Severe worker injury risks and million-dollar vehicle write-offs.
